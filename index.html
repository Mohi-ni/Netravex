DPDP Act in Plain Language

Three Simple Roles in Every Bank

Understanding who owns personal data, who decides how it is used, and who processes it on the bank's instructions.

1

Data Principal

The customer or employee whose personal information is collected—such as KYC documents, phone numbers, account balances, or service tickets.

Empowered with rights to access, correct, erase & withdraw consent
2

Data Fiduciary (The Bank)

The co-operative bank or enterprise deciding why and how personal data is collected, stored, and used to deliver banking services.

Accountable for notices, lawful purpose, security & breach response
3

Data Processor

Any third-party service provider (CBS vendor, SMS gateway, KYC verification agency, cloud backup) processing data strictly on the bank's instructions.

Governed by clear contracts, security clauses & monitoring

The 5-Stage Banking Data Lifecycle

Digital trust is built and verified at every hand-off across your systems.

End-to-End Traceability
01 // COLLECT
Onboarding & KYC

Customer applies or updates personal details at branch or portal.

02 // USE
Service Delivery

Operations & branch teams process records to run accounts.

03 // SHARE
Vendors & Partners

Payment networks, SMS providers, or regulators receive limited data.

04 // STORE
Systems & Backups

Records reside in Core Banking, branch files, and recovery archives.

05 // DELETE
Safe Erasure

Data is securely removed or anonymised once statutory duties end.
